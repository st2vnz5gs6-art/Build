# Library sources

The library holds publicly available SP Energy Networks documents, supplied by the user and downloaded from the
SPEN document library (https://www.spenergynetworks.co.uk/pages/documents.aspx). They are marked "Internal Use"
by SPEN, so they are **not committed to this repository**: place the PDFs in `library/` locally.

| Document | Title | Issue | Scope | URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SUB-03-034 | General Specification for the Civil Engineering and Building Design and Construction of 275kV & 400kV Substations | 5 | 275/400kV substations (primary library document for EGL1 AC compounds) | https://www.spenergynetworks.co.uk/userfiles/file/SUB-03-034.pdf |
| SUB-03-026 | General Specification … of 132kV Grid Substations | 7 | 132kV grid substations | https://www.spenergynetworks.co.uk/userfiles/file/SUB-03-026.pdf |

| SUB-03-017 | General Specification … of Secondary Substations | 9 | Secondary substations | https://www.spenergynetworks.co.uk/userfiles/file/SUB-03-017.pdf |
| SUB-01-012 | Substation Fire Protection Policy | 4 | All SPEN substations | https://www.spenergynetworks.co.uk/userfiles/file/SUB-01-012.pdf |
| SUB-01-018 | Substation Flood Resilience Policy | 3 | All SPEN substations | https://www.spenergynetworks.co.uk/userfiles/file/SUB-01-018.pdf |

URLs follow SPEN's `userfiles/file/<ID>.pdf` pattern. Automated download from the build environment was blocked
by the SPEN website (HTTP 403), so the files were supplied manually.

## Not in the library

* No HVDC converter-station-specific SPEN civil specification is publicly available; SUB-03-034 (275/400kV) is
  the closest match for EGL1 AC compounds. The tool says so when a query falls outside it.
* British/European standards (BS EN 1992, BS 8500, BS EN ISO 1461 …) are copyright and not included. The tool
  names them as "likely governing standard" but never states their requirements.
* Contract-specific requirements (EGL1 Employer's Requirements) can be added to `library/` and are ranked first
  in `hierarchy.json` if their document ID starts `EGL1-`, `ER-` or `CSR-`.

No synthetic specifications were generated: the real documents cover every topic in the brief (foundations,
bunding and oil containment, concrete and reinforcement, steelwork/metalwork, drainage, roads, earthworks,
fencing). Synthetic documents would be marked "DEMO – NOT SPT CONTROLLED DOCUMENT" and flagged in the library view.
